My Tech Journey

My experience throughout Tech Teen Launchpad.

Speak to Your Learning

The topics that stood out to me the most were learning the fundamentals of programming, including HTML, CSS, and Python. These programming languages allowed me to create and experiment with different projects, such as building my own webesite or designing a digital portfolio. Through these experiences, I developed valuable technical and problem-solving skills that will benefit me in my future education and career.

Speak to Your Progress

Throughout this course, I have grown in many ways. At the beginning, I did not know how to write a single line of code, but with the guidance of my instructors, I learned how to write and understand programs comfortably. As I completed more projects, I became more interested in pursuing a career in technology.

Portfolio Development

My strongest projects which I built in class are the "Hidden Figures" and "Creating Your Own Animations." For the "Hidden Figures" project, my group researched Tessy Thomas and learned about her contributions to technology. In my animation project, I used basic CSS and HTML to create a spinning sun, moving car, and a floating balloon which helped me improve my creativity skills.

Future Endeavors

This course helped me discover that I enjoy coding and creating projects using HTML and CSS. Learning them gave me a better understanding of technology and showed me that coding is something I want to continue learning and pursuing. I hope to take more courses and improve my current skills and explore fields like computer science or data science. The projects and classwork I completed in this course inspired me to continue learning about technology.
